About DYCD

Damboa Youth Capacity Development (DYCD) is a youth-focused organization committed to promoting education, leadership, skills development, participation and community development.


Our Vision

To build an empowered, educated and responsible generation of Damboa youth.


Our Mission

To encourage youth participation, promote opportunities and contribute to the development of our community.
